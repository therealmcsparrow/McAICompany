# McSparrow: Company profile

Read only the section you need.

## Identity
- Company: **McSparrow**
- Builds: Own products (SaaS, web apps, native desktop apps for Windows, Linux, macOS) and Client projects.

## Voice presets
Each project's `.company/project.md` picks one in `voice:`. Apply it through `aico:writing`.
- **clear**: friendly and plain. Short sentences, concrete benefits, no hype, no exclamation marks.
- **bold**: playful and punchy. Personality first, witty headlines, still truthful. Never mock the user.
- **technical**: precise and formal. Exact terms, numbers over adjectives, no marketing fluff.

## Legal
- Jurisdiction: Netherlands / EU. GDPR applies; Dutch consumer law for sales.
- Default license for Own products: **proprietary** (all rights reserved).
- Dependencies: no copyleft (GPL/AGPL/LGPL-static) in Own products without CEO approval.
- Client projects: license and jurisdiction come from `.company/project.md`.

## Architecture
Every product is Core + Modules joined by Contracts. Rules: `aico:contracts`.

## Approval
The CEO (the human user) approves at three Gates: Brief, Plan, Ship. See `aico:pipeline`.
