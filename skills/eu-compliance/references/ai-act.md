# AI Act (Art. 50 transparency)

## What applies
Any product with a chatbot, assistant, or AI-generated audio, image, video or text shown to users. Art. 50 applies from 2 Aug 2026.

## Obligations (checklist)
- [ ] Users are told they are interacting with an AI system, unless that is obvious from context.
- [ ] AI-generated audio, image, video and text carries machine-readable marking (metadata or watermark) where technically feasible.
- [ ] Deepfakes (realistic AI-made or altered people, places, events) are disclosed as AI-generated or manipulated.
- [ ] AI-generated text published to inform the public on matters of public interest is disclosed, unless a human reviewed it and takes editorial responsibility.
- [ ] Disclosures are clear, given at first interaction or exposure, and accessible (also to screen readers).
- [ ] House practice, not law: Assets made with AI keep their generation record (tool, prompt, date) beside the source.

Owners: `legal` and `ai-data-engineer`.

## Also check
- Higher-risk uses (hiring, credit, education, biometrics) carry stricter duties. Stop and ask `legal`.
- Personal data in prompts or training is a GDPR matter: `legal`.

## Goes to `## Blocking`
- A user-facing AI feature with no AI disclosure.
- AI-generated media shipped with no marking and no recorded reason.
- A realistic deepfake without disclosure.
