# Writing

1. **Voice**: read `voice:` in `.company/project.md`, then read only that preset in the Company profile (the path in your dispatch, or `../../company/PROFILE.md` from this skill's base directory), under `## Voice presets`.
2. **Write** for one reader with one goal per piece. Lead with the point; specifics over adjectives.
3. **Clean up** with `humanizer` if it's available. Fallback: cut filler, hype, "not X but Y" contrasts, forced lists of three and stock AI phrasing; vary sentence length.
4. Legal text (privacy, terms) comes from the legal Role. Never improvise it.
